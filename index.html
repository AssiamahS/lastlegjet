Questions people actually ask

Is this legal?

Yes — with the right structure. All flights are operated by FAA-certificated Part 135 air carriers. LastLeg acts as an air charter broker under DOT rules (14 CFR Part 295), and per-seat sales are structured to comply with DOT public charter regulations. We never operate aircraft.

What's the catch?

Timing. Last legs follow someone else's schedule — the departure can shift hours or cancel if the jet re-books a full charter. If a leg cancels, you get an instant full refund. This is for flexible travelers, not tight connections.

How cheap is "cheap"?

Whole empty legs typically sell for 25–75% off charter price. Split per seat, a leg that charters for $28,000 can land under $500 a seat on an 8-seat aircraft.

Can I buy the whole jet?

Yes — every listing shows a whole-aircraft price alongside the per-seat price. Buy it out and bring whoever you want.

LastLeg is not a direct air carrier and does not own or operate aircraft. All flights are performed by licensed FAA Part 135 air carriers who exercise full operational control. LastLeg acts as an authorized agent for its clients in arranging air transportation.
